01

Information We Collect

📱 Screen Time Data (Usage Statistics)

GenGhealth uses Android's PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS permission to measure your total daily phone screen time in milliseconds. This is used solely to determine if you have met your daily health goals (under 5h, 6h, or 7h thresholds).

  • Only the total usage duration is processed — never which specific apps you use.
  • This aggregated total (in milliseconds) is synced to our secure Firebase servers to track your daily health goal progress.
  • No individual app names, app usage breakdowns, or browsing activity are ever collected or transmitted.

👤 Personal Account Information

When you register, we collect:

  • Email address — for account creation, login, and reward processing.
  • Display name — shown within the app on your profile.
  • User ID — a unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication.

🏆 Reward & Transaction Data

To operate the rewards system, we store:

  • Daily health goal records (date, total screen time in ms, points earned)
  • Reward withdrawal requests (reward type, points deducted, status)
  • Referral relationships (who referred whom, bonus points)

03

App Usage Statistics Permission

PACKAGE_USAGE_STATSSensitive Permission

GenGhealth requests the App Usage Access permission on Android. Here is exactly what this means for your privacy:

✅
What we DO access: Total screen-on duration across all apps for the current day (a single number in milliseconds).
❌
What we do NOT access: Which specific apps you use, how long you spend in each app, your browsing history, or any app content.
❌
We do NOT: Sell, share, or disclose your usage data to any third party for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

A full disclosure dialog is displayed within the app before this permission is ever requested. You can revoke this permission at any time in your Android Settings → Apps → Special App Access → Usage Access.
